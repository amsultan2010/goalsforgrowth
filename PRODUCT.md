# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite multi-page build with a hand-written HTML file per route. The pages have to ship as real HTML, motion comes from one GSAP module, and two pages do not need a React app.

## Users

Anyone who wants to know what Goals for Growth is. They read what the program is, who it is for, what a week looks like, and then the photos. No signup, no contact, no ads.

## Product Purpose

A public record of Goals for Growth, Taimur Naeem's football and mentorship weeks in public schools for children aged 8 to 12 (grades 3 to 7). Success is a visitor understanding the program and seeing the real weeks.

## Positioning

It documents weeks that already happened in two public schools. It is not a club that takes signups.

## Operating Context

The program is a school visit: a welcome day, then dribbling, passing, shooting, and mini matches. After the visit, Taimur's plan in the slideshow is video drills and challenges. The site only covers the visits and the press, because that is what the files show.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two public routes: `https://goalsforgrowth.site/` and `https://goalsforgrowth.site/gallery`.
- Home hero is a looping video. The file is `combined_4clips_v2.mp4` (given from Downloads, not in the project folder yet).
- Gallery groups are QAPS, NJV, and Winter 2025. Full school names were not given.
- Say that Taimur Naeem runs it. Do not say he is the U15 FIFA World Cup participant. The one-to-one sessions with a U15 FIFA World Cup participant stay an unnamed fact from the brief.
- 300+ kids coached across two public schools. Multiple newspaper pieces. Government workers appear in that coverage.
- No contact, no ads, no form.
- Motion stays light: a few scroll moments, not a full motion catalog.
- Deploy target is undecided. The live origin to use in links is `https://goalsforgrowth.site`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Goals for Growth. The slideshow line under it is "Empowering Youth Through Football and Education".
- The page should feel comfortable, spacious, and easy on the eyes.
- Structure reference only: the section order of elitesports.sa. Not its look.
- Colors come from `GFG Description.pdf`, not from a new palette. Sampled from the slides: kit green `#4BB84A`, jersey red `#CD2813`, gold `#F8D838`, warm ink `#3F403E`, paper `#FCFCFB`. Green, red, and gold do the work. Ink and paper are the deck's own near-black and off-white.

## Evidence on Hand

- Slideshow and program copy: `GFG Description.pdf`.
- Photos and one camp video, already grouped: `QAPS 2025/`, `NJV 2025/`, `Winter 2025 /`.
- Winter 2025 includes Daily Dawn and Daily Pakistan Express pages (26 Dec 2025). They are coverage of this program. A short excerpt, translated, sits beside those images. Do not invent lines that are not in the clipping.
- Do not invent testimonials, counts, school names, or a named World Cup player.
- Children's faces in the provided photos may be published.

## Product Principles

- Say what the weeks were, in the words the slideshow already uses.
- Proof is the photos and the newspaper pages, not claims without a file.
- The site explains. It does not ask for anything.
- Two pages: the story, then the pictures.
- The slideshow's colors are the colors.
