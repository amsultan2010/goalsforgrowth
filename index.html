The week on site

Empowering youth through football and education.

Five school days for children aged 8 to 12, grades 3 to 7. Drills, games, and a talk with the students and staff.

  1. Welcome

    The program is introduced to the students and the staff.

    Icebreakers are a pass with your name, and tag drills. Then a short talk: sport in life and in learning, and how to go about football if you decide to take it seriously.

  2. Dribbling

    Dribbling and control.

    Tight spaces, a change of direction, and the speed of play.

  3. Passing

    Passing and team play.

    Communication, scanning, how to strike a pass, and which pass fits the moment.

  4. Shooting

    Shooting techniques.

    Which part of the foot, different ways to shoot, then finishing practice.

  5. Mini matches

    The skills from the week, played in a game.

    A competitive end to the five days.

  • 300+ kids coached
  • 2 public schools
  • 8 to 12 years old
  • 3 to 7 grades

How a day is run

  • Team points. Points each day for the skill and for sportsmanship. Teams can win awards for effort, growth, and leadership.
  • Junior coaches. Older students help plan drills and guide the younger children.
  • Journals. Kids write their goals, what was hard, and what got better.

Who runs it

Taimur Naeem runs the weeks. With the group days, there are one-to-one sessions with a U15 FIFA World Cup participant.

The weeks put football in front of children this age, and make determination, resilience, and hard work something they practice. The aim on the program sheet is community interest in football and well-being.

After the visit

After the first school week, the plan is regular videos: drills and sessions students can do, stories from the game, and challenges with prizes and shoutouts.

Students and staff get a short feedback form. Drills are checked on the first day and the last. A football activity guide stays with the school, with the idea of an after-school club.

In the papers

Daily Dawn (Lahore and Multan), Daily Pakistan Express, and Daily Khabrain Times (Islamabad) printed the visit on 26 December 2025. Government workers attended.
