In the papers

Daily Dawn (Lahore and Multan), Daily Pakistan Express, and Daily Khabrain Times (Islamabad) printed the school visit on 26 December 2025. Government workers attended.

The newspaper pages

300+ kids

Goals for Growth is Taimur Naeem's football weeks in public schools. QAPS, NJV, and a winter session. The children are 8 to 12, in grades 3 to 7.

Some of the coaching is one-to-one with a U15 FIFA World Cup participant.

What they do

A week on the ball. They learn it at their feet, pass it, shoot, and finish in a game. The aim is a feel for football, and a reason to keep playing.

  • Welcome. The school hears what the week is.
  • Dribbling. The ball at their feet, in a tight space.
  • Passing. They play it to each other and look up.
  • Shooting. They finish.
  • Mini matches. The week ends in a game.
