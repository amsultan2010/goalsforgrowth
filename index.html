Goals for Growth

300+ kids coached

Goals for Growth is a Pakistan-based community outreach initiative helping underprivileged students get access to sports.

Some of the coaching is one-to-one with a U15 FIFA World Cup participant.

In the papers

Goals for Growth was covered in national newspapers such as Daily Dawn, Daily Pakistan Express, and Daily Khabrain Times.

What they do

A week on the ball. They learn it at their feet, pass it, shoot, and finish in a game. The aim is a feel for football, and a reason to keep playing.
